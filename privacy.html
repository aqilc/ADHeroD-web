ADHeroD privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

What we store

When you sign in, your tasks, notes, settings and chat messages are stored in our database (Supabase) so they sync between your devices. Signed out, everything stays in your browser.

Files

Files you attach to a task or a chat are stored in ADHeroD's own Google Drive folder. Only you and the people in that chat can open them. A file nothing points to anymore is deleted after 90 days.

Google data

In a Drive, ADHeroD can only open files it created itself.

If you connect Google Calendar, ADHeroD reads your calendars and their events and keeps a copy in our database, so Plan shows them on every device you sign in on. It also keeps an encrypted Google sign-in token so it can keep syncing. You can cut it off any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google data is used only for these things, never sold, shared or used for ads, and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use rules.

Who sees your data

You do, and the friends you chat with see that chat. We don't sell data or show ads, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Email aqilcmp@gmail.com and we'll delete your account, tasks, messages and files.
