ADHeroD privacy

Last updated October 8, 2026.

What we store

When you sign in, your tasks, notes, settings and chat messages are stored in our database (Supabase) so they sync between your devices. Signed out, everything stays in your browser.

Files

Files you attach to a task or a chat are stored in ADHeroD's own Google Drive folder. Only you and the people in that chat can open them. A file nothing points to anymore is deleted after 90 days.

Google data

ADHeroD asks Google only for access to files it created itself. It can't see anything else in a Drive. Google data is used only to store and serve those files, never sold, shared or used for ads, and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use rules.

Who sees your data

You do, and the friends you chat with see that chat. We don't sell data or show ads, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Email aqilcmp@gmail.com and we'll delete your account, tasks, messages and files.
